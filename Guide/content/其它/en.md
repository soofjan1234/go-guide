# HR 面试问题分类准备

## 1. 基础背景类

### 1.1 Please introduce yourself.

面试官你好，我叫侯汶政。

过去我主要负责后端服务开发、接口设计、数据库和缓存相关功能，也参与过从 0 到 1 的项目建设。

平时我也会使用 AI 工具提升开发效率，比如辅助写代码、整理文档、定位问题和做代码审查。

我对远程工作也比较适应，因为我习惯主动同步进度、把问题描述清楚，并且对结果负责。希望有机会加入你们团队，谢谢。

English answer:

Hello, my name is Wenzheng Hou. 

In my previous work, I mainly focused on backend service development, API design, database and cache-related features, and I also participated in building projects from zero to one.

I also use AI tools in my daily work to improve efficiency, such as coding, documentation, debugging, and code review.

I think I am suitable for remote work because I am used to proactively updating progress, explaining problems clearly, and taking responsibility for results. I hope I can have the opportunity to join your team. Thank you.

### 1.2 Resume walkthrough

- Can you walk me through your resume? / Can you briefly summarize your work experience? / What are your main responsibilities in your current or previous role?
- What kind of projects have you worked on?
- What is your strongest backend development experience?
- What are your main technical strengths?
- What is your biggest weakness?
- What is your biggest achievement in your previous work?
- What is the most difficult problem you solved?

## 2. 求职动机类

- Why are you exploring new opportunities? / Why are you looking for a new job now? / Why did you leave your previous company?
- What are you looking for in your next role? /What are your career goals for the next few years? / Where do you see yourself in 3 years?

## 3. 公司意向类

- Why do you want to join us specifically? / Why are you interested in our company? / What attracts you most about this role?
- What do you know about BJAK or our new group A1?
- What do you think about our business direction?
- Why do you want to join a startup or fast-growing company?
- What kind of company do you want to join?

## 4. 远程工作类

- Why are you interested in remote work?
- Have you worked remotely before?
- What do you think is the biggest challenge of remote work?
- What is the difference between remote work and office work?
- How do you keep communication efficient in a remote team?
- How do you make sure your manager knows your progress?
- How do you handle communication delays in remote work? / How do you avoid information gaps in remote work? / How do you handle timezone differences?
- What would you do if you cannot reach a teammate but the issue is urgent?
- How do you stay productive when working from home?

## 5. 主动性 / Owner 意识类

- Tell me about a time you proactively solved a problem. / Tell me about a time you found a problem before others noticed it.
- What do you do when requirements are unclear?
- What would you do if a task is outside your responsibility?
- How do you handle blockers?
- How do you move a task forward when nobody gives clear instructions?
- Do you prefer waiting for tasks or finding problems yourself?
- How do you take ownership of a project?
- What does ownership mean to you?
- How do you make sure a task is truly completed?
- How do you follow up after reporting a problem?

## 6. 创业团队适应类

- Can you accept a fast-paced startup environment?
- Can you accept overtime when there is an urgent delivery?
- How do you handle changing priorities? / What would you do if the company direction changes quickly?
- Are you comfortable wearing multiple hats?
- How do you balance speed and quality?
- How do you handle pressure?
- How do you adapt to a team with limited resources?
- What kind of startup culture do you like? / What do you think is important in an early-stage or fast-growing team?

## 7. 沟通协作类

- How do you communicate when you are blocked?
- How do you handle disagreement with teammates? / Tell me about a time you had a conflict with a teammate.
- How do you give feedback to others?
- How do you receive feedback?
- How do you work with product managers?
- How do you work with QA or testers?
- How do you work with designers or frontend developers?
- What kind of people do you like working with?
- Tell me the people and style you dislike working with / What kind of working style is hard for you to work with?
- How do you handle a teammate who does not communicate clearly?
- How do you make sure everyone is aligned?

## 8. 优先级和执行力类

- How do you prioritize multiple tasks? 
- What would you do if everything is urgent? 
- How do you manage deadlines? / How do you make sure important tasks are delivered on time? /How do you break down a large task? 
- Tell me about a time you handled pressure.  
- How do you estimate task complexity? 
- How do you report progress and risks? 

## 9. 学习能力和成长类

- How do you learn a new technology? / How do you learn a new business domain?
- How do you improve yourself as a developer? / How do you keep up with backend technology?
- What areas do you want to improve next? / What is your learning plan for the next year?
- Tell me about something new you learned recently.
- How do you handle tasks involving unfamiliar code?
- How do you quickly understand a new project?
- How do you learn from mistakes?

## 10. AI 和工程效率类

- How do you use AI in your daily work?
- What is your view on AI's impact on software development?
- Do you think AI will replace developers?
- Do you rely on AI too much?
- How do you verify AI-generated code? / How do you make sure AI-generated answers are correct?
- What kinds of tasks do you use AI for?
- What kinds of tasks should not fully rely on AI?
- How does AI improve your development efficiency?
- Have you used AI for debugging or code review?

## 11. 价值观 / 工作风格类

- What kind of working environment do you prefer?
- What kind of manager do you like?
- What kind of team culture do you value?
- What motivates you at work?
- What makes you frustrated at work?
- How do you define a good engineer?
- What does teamwork mean to you?
- What does responsibility mean to you?
- What kind of company culture is not suitable for you?
- How do you handle repetitive or boring tasks?
- What do you care about most in a job?

## 12. 稳定性 / 现实条件类

- What is your expected salary?
- When can you start?
- What is your notice period?
- Are you interviewing with other companies?
- Do you have any offers now?
- Are you comfortable with contractor employment?
- Are you comfortable with remote or cross-border employment?
- Are you comfortable communicating in English?
- Are you comfortable with flexible working hours?
- Are you available for meetings in different time zones?
- What are your long-term career plans?

## 13. 行为面试类

- Tell me about a time you failed.
- Tell me about a time you made a mistake.
- Tell me about a time you disagreed with your manager.
- Tell me about a time you had to learn something quickly.
- Tell me about a time you worked under pressure.
- Tell me about a time you improved a process.
- Tell me about a time you helped a teammate.
- Tell me about a time you received negative feedback.
- Tell me about a time you handled an urgent production issue.
- Tell me about a time you took responsibility for a result.

## 14. 反问公司类

- How does the team usually collaborate remotely?
- Do you have daily meetings, task management tools, or documentation practices?
- How does the team handle urgent issues or production incidents?
