## Style Prompt

Clean technical teaching video on a warm off-white canvas, with precise diagram callouts, restrained motion, and a calm interview-prep pace. The page should feel like a polished runtime whiteboard: readable, structured, and quietly technical.

## Colors

- Canvas: `#f4f0e6`
- Ink: `#17211d`
- Muted ink: `#53615b`
- Accent green: `#1f7a5b`
- Accent amber: `#d69028`
- Code surface: `#0f1815`

## Typography

- Headline: `HF Headline`, declared with `local("Arial Black")`
- Body: `HF Serif`, declared with `local("Songti SC")`
- Code and runtime labels: `HF Mono`, declared with `local("Menlo")`

## Motion

- Primary transition: medium blur crossfade, 0.55s.
- Entrance motion: small y-shift, x-shift, scale, and opacity combinations.
- Ambient motion: subtle grid drift and diagram breathing only.

## What NOT To Do

- Do not use dark neon, purple-blue gradients, or marketing hero styling.
- Do not show long paragraphs on screen.
- Do not make scene changes as hard cuts.
- Do not use tiny labels under 20px.
- Do not cover the diagram with captions.
